Every complaint that comes in needs to be triaged. Is it a defect? What's the risk? Does it need a CAPA? For small quality teams, that process is manual, time-consuming, and inconsistent.

Four steps. One panel. No spreadsheets.

1

Defect Classification

Determines whether the issue represents a deviation from a User Need or Product Requirement. NON-DEFECTs are flagged immediately.

2

Probability Assessment

Scores the likelihood that users encounter this defect during normal use, using your configured 1–5 scale.

3

Severity Assessment

Scores the potential harm if the defect occurs — grounded in patient safety and therapeutic impact, not just software severity.

4

Risk Score

Combines probability and severity against your configured 5×5 risk matrix to produce a risk level and recommended disposition.

Fully configurable

Your product requirements, user needs, probability scale, severity scale, and risk matrix are all defined in the Settings panel. Nothing is hardcoded.

Evidence citations

Every conclusion comes with citations — the specific comments or fields that influenced the classification. Audit-ready by design.

Stays in Jira

No context switching. The panel runs directly on the issue. Results can be auto-posted as a formatted comment on the ticket.

ISO 14971-aligned

The four-step pipeline maps directly to the risk management process required for SaMD under ISO 14971.
